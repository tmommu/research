How the boat and flood node are wired

Orange lines carry 12 V power, teal lines carry 5 V, dashed lines carry signals. The motors run on fused 12 V branches of their own, so a motor surge cannot reset a board.

  • Solar chain. Panel, charge controller and battery on the boat. The controller cuts the load when the battery is low.
  • Thrusters and belt. Each has its own fuse and a driver that stays off until the board enables it. A current sensor on the belt detects jams.
  • Boards. One buck converter feeds the ESP32-S3 and the ESP32-CAM. A second feeds the servo and LED ring, so their spikes cannot reset the boards.
  • Flood node. A separate small solar node with three float switches and a buzzer. It tells the boat to stay docked from the alert level.
