SAGIP · autonomous solar skimmer boat (draft)

It spots debris, goes to it and collects it

  1. 1Bow camera: spots floating debris ahead
  2. 2Scoop: lifts debris onto the belt
  3. 3Cleated conveyor belt: carries items up in a single layer
  4. 4Belt camera and CNN: names each item under a lit hood
  5. 5Servo gate: plastic to Bin A, plants or unsure to Bin B
  6. 6Bins A and B: held on board, emptied at the dock
  7. 7Electronics box: two ESP32-S3 boards, drivers, converters
  8. 830 W solar panel: flat roof over the deck, charges the battery
  9. 912 V battery: powers thrusters, belt and boards
  10. 10Thruster: steers and drives the boat
  11. 11Twin floats and deck: keep it afloat and stable
  12. 12Side sensors and compass: follow the bank, turn at barriers
  1. 1Patrol: follows the bank between two barriers
  2. 2Detect: the bow camera spots floating debris
  3. 3Approach and scoop: steers to it and lifts it onto the belt
  4. 4Resume: returns to the bank path
  5. 5Dock: unload bins when full or the battery is low
  6. 6Flood node: three float switches and a buzzer, boat stays docked from the alert level
