## 4. Current design (self-navigating skimmer boat plus a flood node)
- **Title (formal, fits two lines on the title slide, about 66 characters max):** "SAGIP: Autonomous Solar Vessel for Plastic Recovery and Monitoring". Longer titles run to three lines and overlap the theme text.
- **Boat:** twin 6-inch PVC floats (one 1.5 m pipe each, **no couplers**) with foam in the bow and stern, 1.4 m deck, 30 W panel flat on the deck, 12 V 12 Ah sealed lead-acid battery low between floats, motors on fused branches with drivers that default to off, tilt cut-off, **a stop switch**.
- **Size (planning values from the 3D model):** about 1.7 m long, 0.85 m wide, 0.55 m high, draft about 10 cm. **Weight: the entry says about 30 kg** (an estimate between 25 and about 32 kg empty, orange pipe is about 2.5 kg per meter). Be ready to add a third float. Float and tilt tests decide.
- **Navigation (decided Oct 31, changed this session):** the boat **navigates on its own** along a preset route between two floating barriers about 30 m apart, two thrusters (differential steering), two side ultrasonic sensors keep it off the bank, bump switches cover the bow, it docks when the bins are full or the battery is low and stays docked from the flood alert level. **The phone remote and the compass were removed to cut cost.** Add a compass back only if pond tests show drift.
- **Two fixed cameras:** navigation and detection = ESP32-CAM Type C at the bow (OV2640, 4 MB PSRAM, USB-C, Makerlab PHP 599). Sorting = ESP32-S3 N16R8 camera board under a lit hood (Shopee, about PHP 345 to 530). The bow camera could be a second S3 board (about PHP 250 cheaper) but the user chose the ESP32-CAM.
- **Detect and scoop, count and sort, plastic accounting, flood node, deep sleep:** unchanged. Bow camera runs an INT8 object detector, a scoop lifts plastic onto a cleated conveyor, the S3 camera runs a 2D CNN (INT8, TFLite Micro), a servo gate sends plastic to Bin A and plants, mixed or unsure items to Bin B. Counts become estimated kg and junk-shop value. The flood node has three float switches, buzzer and LED, 2 W panel, protected 18650 in an IP67 box. Deep sleep values (180 mA awake, 6 mA asleep at 5 V) are datasheet-style, not measured, and the entry says so.
- **Site:** Dario River, Quezon City. **The entry no longer names a barangay, household count or cleanup program** (see section 8).
- History: earlier versions (rake-arm bank unit, hybrid floating foot, floating raft) are in `old_versions/` on the older branch. The user rejected a heat device, a dengue "Lamok Lens" idea and the PHP 6,000 first design.
