## Rules

- No student enters the water; adults empty the bin.
- Buy only the stage you are on.
- Keep receipts; write real prices in the "Actual PHP" column.
- If a price is far above the estimate, tell the Lead before buying; adjust from the contingency or choose a cheaper part.
